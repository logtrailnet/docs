## Built for the AI Era

Logtrail is designed to be the easiest logging platform to integrate using AI agents like **Cursor**, **Windsurf**, and **Claude Engineer**.

*   **Zero-Config AI Discovery**: AI tools automatically understand our API surface via structured metadata.
*   **Agentic Search**: LCQL's natural syntax allows agents to construct complex filters without writing raw SQL.
*   **Automated Validation**: Use the `/validate` endpoint to let your AI tools verify ingestion payloads before they hit production.
