*Last Updated: February 14, 2026*

At Logtrail, we take your privacy seriously. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your data.

## 1. Data We Collect
*   **Account Information**: Name, email, and billing details provided during signup.
*   **Log Data**: Technical logs ingested via our API/SDKs.
*   **Usage Metadata**: Information about how you interact with our dashboard and API for performance monitoring.

## 2. How We Use Data
*   To provide and maintain the Logtrail service.
*   To notify you about changes to your account or plan limits.
*   To detect and prevent technical issues or security breaches.

## 3. Data Retention and Deletion
*   Log data is retained according to your plan's retention period (e.g., 30 days).
*   Once retention expires, data is permanently deleted from our active storage.
*   Upon account termination, all associated data is purged within 30 days.

## 4. Security
We use industry-standard encryption (AES-256 at rest, TLS in transit) to protect your data. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

## 5. Contact Us
For any privacy-related questions, please contact us at `privacy@logtrail.net`.
